# Art credits

## Market tileset (assets-src/PathAndObjects.png)
LPC-style tiles and props by **Zabin**, **Jetrel**, **Redshrike**, **Hyptosis**, **Daneeklu** and **Bertram**,
with recolourations by Zabin (see assets-src/Artistinfo2.png for who made which piece).

LPC art on OpenGameArt is normally released under CC-BY-SA 3.0 and/or GPL 3.0. Confirm the exact licence on the
page you downloaded it from. Both require this credit, and CC-BY-SA requires modified versions to be shared under
the same licence. The files in client/public/assets/ (atlas.png, ground.png) are modified versions: recoloured stall
awnings, trimmed props, and a baked ground map.
